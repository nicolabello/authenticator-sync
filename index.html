Google Authenticator

  1. In the app: open the menu → Transfer accounts → Export accounts, select all, Next.
  2. Easiest: click Scan with webcam and show it each QR code in turn.
  3. Or screenshot every QR code (there may be several), copy them to this computer and drop them here. Delete the screenshots afterwards — they contain your secrets.
